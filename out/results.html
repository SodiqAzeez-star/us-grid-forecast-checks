Electricity load forecast checks: results of this run

Run on 2026-11-20 (UTC). Test data 2025-11-01 to 2026-10-31; audit data 2023-07 to 2026-10. Public files from the US Energy Information Administration (Form EIA-930).

The grid operators' own forecasts beat a simple model on PJM, ERCOT, MISO and NYISO.

On the same test year, a simple model (LightGBM, a standard tree model) with a temperature forecast missed by 3.71 percent on average across those four grids, against 2.45 percent for the operators' own forecasts. Without weather it missed by 5.66 percent. CAISO is shown but not compared, because its stored forecast runs far below its reported demand at midday for a reason not yet confirmed.

4 of 55 balancing authorities show a timing mismatch between their stored forecast and their demand.

Balancing authorities are the bodies that balance each grid area. Each was checked month by month: the stored forecast was moved by up to three hours either way, and a month counts when the best move cuts the error by at least 10 percent. Found: PJM, California ISO (CISO), Arizona Public Service (AZPS), South Carolina Public Service Authority (SC). This shows the two columns disagree in time, not which one is mislabelled. The check uses the public federal EIA file; the Arizona and South Carolina cases are smaller and unconfirmed.
